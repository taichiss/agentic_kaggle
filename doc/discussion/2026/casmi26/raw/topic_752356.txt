Our changes



Depth 7 (two lines). On older bases it had given +0.003 and +0.004. On this base it lost 0.008, so the engine-2 rankers here look already tuned around depth 6.

Candidate-level PubChem gate. After the ICEBERG/GLACIER re-order, the PubChem candidate at rank 1 can differ from the one whose channel-level S / popularity / margin the promotion gate used. We recompute the gate on the promoted candidate's own evidence, thresholds unchanged, and write an audit CSV of which decisions changed.

Features-frozen generation ("genfix"). Engine 1's ranker uses list-relative features (ranks, z-scores, gap to best, softmax, formula share), so adding generated candidates re-scores the originals; we measured their top 5 reshuffling in roughly half of molecules. Genfix keeps the original engine-1 run exactly, runs a second wider generation (20 parents, 100 per parent, 500 total), and inserts only structures the first run lacks, on the first run's score scale minus a penalty (δ = 2). The originals cannot be re-scored.


A bench that sees the problem

On a "thin class-1" bench (class-1 answers keep only 1-5 library spectra), ungated gen20 pushed the correct answer off rank 1 in 18 of 270 molecules (6.7%); genfix did so in 0 of 270. This is the first bench of ours that sees the failure that cost ungated gen20 −0.010 on the leaderboard. None of our benches is calibrated to the leaderboard, so we use it as a safety check, not a forecast.

What the field is doing

From dariushafshar's public-chain table (481 versions): the leaders are one lineage. The big repeatable steps (+0.008 each) all came from giving the ICEBERG/GLACIER simulators evidence on more molecules (N1A proxies, the N1 challenger). PubChem promotion thresholds bought +0.006 once. Around 20 other tweaks (gate thresholds, a sixth slot, LOTUS and popularity weights) were flat.

Would we reach gold?

Gold (about 0.471) is roughly one more molecule on the public set, since one molecule at rank 1 is worth about +0.0076. With what we have we expect to fall short. The levers we can see are: (1) forward-model coverage for molecules that still lack ICEBERG/GLACIER scores; (2) retraining the engine-1 ranker with source flags (the real class-3 lever, but 10-40 CPU hours per setting); (3) a neural re-rank inside the top 25. We put our odds of 0.471 this month at low.

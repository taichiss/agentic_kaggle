Hello hosts,

Thank you for the detailed answers so far (748065, 745841, 742991). Below is a consolidated list of points that are still open across several threads, ordered by how much they affect solution design. A short yes/no on each is enough.

A. Reproducibility and compliance




Officially released checkpoints. You confirmed that the MassSpecGym-trained GLACIER/ICEBERG checkpoints (coleygroup/ms-pred) and DreaMS are fine. For these, is it sufficient for a winning team to deliver the exact released checkpoint, a pointer to the authors' public training code and data, and our own complete fine-tuning and inference code? Or must the team also re-run the pretraining itself? For DreaMS, does the approval refer to the self-supervised backbone (ssl_model.ckpt)?


Retraining a shared architecture. Following 748065 (1): if a team retrains a participant-shared model architecture itself on allowed data, with code it delivers, and its two selected submissions use only those retrained weights, is that sufficient? And can you confirm that the compliance review covers only the two selected submissions?


B. Specific models and data




TabPFN. Is TabPFN v2 (Prior Labs License: Apache 2.0 with an attribution requirement; pretrained on synthetic tabular tasks only) eligible as a ranking component? Separately, are TabPFN 2.5 and later eligible, given that their weights carry a non-commercial license?


FRIGID (746430, still open). Can the official FRIGID checkpoints on Zenodo (record 19685145, CC BY-NC 4.0; diffusion decoder plus MIST encoders) be used as released, or must they be retrained with the Apache-2.0 code on allowed data?


PubChem-derived counts and natural-product databases. Does the PubChem approval (741857) also cover public CID-PMID, CID-Patent and CID-SID counts used as ranking priors, not only structure subsets? Are LOTUS and NPAtlas fine as structure sources under their own open licenses, without separate approval by name?


C. Data and scoring




Scorer behaviour (748029, still open). Under first-match MRR, appending valid, distinct guesses after an unchanged rank-1 guess should never lower a row's score. Is that guaranteed in the live scorer, or are there row-level timeouts, limits or validation steps that could make a longer list score lower?


Electron-mass correction (746954). Was the correction applied to the hidden test also applied to train.parquet and to the public Enveda-180 Zenodo release, or only to the rerun test? Which ion modes and fields were affected, so that teams do not apply it twice?


Public/private split. Without disclosing class proportions: is the 31% / 69% public/private split stratified in any way, for example by novelty class?


Thank you!
